# Why wrongmark exists

## The 2400-year-old warning

Socrates warned that writing "will introduce forgetfulness into the soul of those who learn it"
and give students "the appearance of wisdom, not true wisdom" (*Phaedrus*, ~370 BC).
Every leap in how knowledge is stored — writing, print, search — has raised the same fear,
and every time, humanity adapted by pairing the new tool with a new discipline:
rhetoric schools for writing, indexes for print, search literacy for the web.

**What is new now is not memory being offloaded — it is judgment.**
The entity at the frontier is an AI that answers eagerly, agrees readily,
and never grades *you*. The teacher didn't retire; the position was quietly abolished.

## The evidence

- MIT Media Lab's EEG study (*Your Brain on ChatGPT*, 2025): the LLM-assisted group showed
  the weakest neural connectivity of all three groups, and **83% could not quote a single
  sentence from the essay they had "just written"**. The authors call the accumulated
  effect *cognitive debt*.
- Gerlich (2025, *Societies*): AI-tool usage correlates with lower critical thinking,
  with **cognitive offloading as the mediating variable** — the more you hand over, the less you exercise.
- Anthropic's own usage research: delegating to AI **without engagement** costs roughly
  **17% of learning** compared to working unaided.
- Roediger & Karpicke (2006, *Psychological Science*): the **testing effect** — one week out,
  recall practice retained ~61% vs ~40% for re-reading. Effortful retrieval is the strongest
  known memory stabilizer, which is exactly what AI use removes from the loop.

## The two principles

1. **Restore the loop.** Judgment only grows through *mistake → pointed out → revised*.
   wrongmark automates the missing teacher: your agent logs what it corrected about you,
   quizzes you before work, and watches the spots where you fall.
2. **One artifact, two readers.** A card is simultaneously your spaced-repetition
   curriculum and the agent's watchlist of your blind spots. This is the line that
   separates wrongmark from both Anki (which reviews you but has no AI) and AI-memory
   layers like mem0 (which remember *for* the AI, deepening the offload):
   **the AI reads your errors to catch you — never to answer for you.**
   That rule is not a suggestion; it is written into the skill as a red line.

## What wrongmark is not

It is not anti-AI. Delegation is fine — unmeasured, unreviewed, uncorrected delegation is not.
The goal is a curve: your calibration going up while the agent's hints go down,
dependence paid off measurably. The tool succeeds when you need it less.
